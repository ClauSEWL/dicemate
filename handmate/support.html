How do I play?

Chess where the cards in your hand decide which piece moves. Draw a card each turn and play up to two: one piece card (a move) and one tactic. Capture the king to win; there is no check or mate. The in-app rules explain every card.

Online play and my account

Online play uses a guest account or Sign in with Apple, shared with our game DiceMate. To delete it: Play online → Delete account.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. To restore them on a new device, use Restore Purchases in the app. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
